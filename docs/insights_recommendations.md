# Management Insights and Recommendations

Prepared for TacoGo leadership. Pages 1 to 3 describe a filtered view (five restaurants, five 2025 months: January, February, March, July, September). Page 4 covers all 1,000 orders from January 2024 to December 2025. Figures marked *derived* are calculated from values shown in the report.

## Summary for Leadership

1. **We have a demand problem, not a pricing or delivery-speed problem.** 2025 closed with 302 orders against 698 in 2024 (-56.7%). Revenue per order and tip rate held steady, so the $2,712.75 revenue gap is almost entirely lost volume.
2. **The filtered pages understate the fall** (-47.5% orders) and overstate how concentrated it is. Two restaurants carry 56% of the filtered volume, yet in the full dataset no restaurant holds more than 11.7%.
3. **Delivery looks fine on average and poor in places.** Average delivery is about 50 minutes, but city-restaurant averages run from 22 to 78 minutes.
4. **Decisions requested:** approve a root-cause review of the lost 396 orders across all ten restaurants, a 45-minute delivery standard by city, and a Large-size upsell test.

## Page 4: Recommendation Report (full dataset, 1,000 orders)
![Redcommendation](assets/Recommendation_Report.PNG)
### What the page shows
1,000 orders, $6,908.25 revenue, $1,806.11 tips, 50.9 min average delivery over 13.1 km. Monthly orders (pooled across both years) open at 113 in January, peak at 119 in March, fall to 66 in June and end at 52 in December.

### Insights

1. **The decline is larger than the YoY cards suggest.** The cards read +43.3% (orders), +43.6% (revenue) and +45.6% (tips) because they compare the two-year total with 2024. Using the Prev and YTD cards as the two years:

   | | 2024 | 2025 | Change |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Orders | 698 | 302 | -56.7% |
   | Revenue | $4,810.50 | $2,097.75 | -56.4% |
   | Tips | $1,240.15 | $565.96 | -54.4% |
   | Revenue per order | $6.89 | $6.95 | +0.8% |
   | Tip rate | 25.8% | 27.0% | +1.2 pts |
   | Avg delivery | 51.08 min | 50.59 min | -1.0% |
   | Avg distance | 13.04 km | 13.14 km | +0.8% |

   *All derived.* Volume accounts for about $2,729 of the $2,712.75 revenue gap, with price and mix adding about $16. Customers who still order spend and tip as before. There are simply fewer of them.

2. **The portfolio is evenly spread, so this is not one failing restaurant.** Orders per restaurant range from 117 (Urban Tacos) to 85 (El Taco Loco), a 1.38x gap, with the other eight between 91 and 106. Taco types sit between 182 (Beef, 18.2%) and 218 (Chicken, 21.8%). A loss of this size across a balanced base points to market-wide or platform-level causes (availability, visibility, competition), not a single menu or outlet.

3. **Seasonality is visible but cannot yet be separated from the 2025 decline.** The monthly line pools two years: March (119) to June (66) is -44.5%, and March to December (52) is -56.3%. A line per year is needed to know how much of the late-year drop is seasonal and how much is the 2025 trend.

4. **Large tacos are the stronger revenue engine.** Large contributes about $3.8K against about $3.0K for Regular across the full dataset. In the filtered view, Large earns about $7.78 per order against $5.65 for Regular.

5. **Delivery performance is healthy on average.** 3.85 minutes per km in 2025 against 3.92 in 2024 (-1.7%). The risk sits in the city spread (see Page 3), not the headline.

### Recommendations

| # | Recommendation | Why | Measure of success |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Re-base the YoY cards on 2025 vs 2024 and add a year split to the monthly chart | The current cards show growth while the narrative describes decline | Cards and narrative agree |
| 2 | Run a 2025 vs 2024 order comparison for all ten restaurants and by city | The 396 lost orders are not yet attributed | Top five contributors named |
| 3 | Check platform-side causes first: listing status, opening hours, search ranking, promotions run in 2024 but not 2025 | A balanced base losing 57% points to a common cause | Cause identified within one reporting cycle |
| 4 | Target a 25% recovery of the gap (about 99 orders) over the next quarter | About $688 revenue and $186 tips at 2025 rates | 99 incremental orders |
| 5 | Protect the troughs with a promotion calendar for June and December | Pooled June is 66 and December 52 against a March peak of 119 | Narrower peak-to-trough gap by year |
| 6 | Make Large the default offer on high-volume items | Large drives the larger share of revenue | Large share of orders and revenue per order |
| 7 | Add food and delivery cost to the model | "Profit" cannot be measured today | Margin by restaurant and size |

## Page 1: Executive Performance Dashboard (filtered)
![Dashboard](assets/DashBoard.png)
### Insights

- **Volume and value both fell, value slightly faster.** Orders 64 vs 122 (-47.5%), revenue $418 vs $830.50 (-49.7%). Revenue per order slipped from $6.81 to $6.52 (-4.2%, *derived*).
- **The monthly shape is two peaks and a collapse.** 28 (January), 1 (February), 24 (March), 4 (July), 7 (September). January and March deliver 81% of orders and about 80% of revenue. February dropped 96% against January.
- **Two restaurants carry the slice.** Spicy Taco House 20 orders (31%), El Taco Loco 16 (25%), Urban Tacos 12 (19%), Grande Tacos 9 (14%), Taco Time Express 7 (11%).
- **Menu mix is balanced.** Veggie 16 (25%), Pork 14 (21.88%), Chicken 13 (20.31%), Beef 12 (18.75%), Fish 9 (14.06%).
- **Large earns more per order.** About $210 from 27 Large orders and $209 from 37 Regular orders.
- **Tips are eroding faster than sales.** $109.01 vs $238.41 (-54.3%), about 26 cents per revenue dollar.

### Recommendations

1. **Audit February and July to September.** Pull restaurant availability and listing logs by day. The February result (1 order against 28 in January) looks like an outage or delisting rather than a demand dip.
2. **Set a floor of 26 orders a month** for this slice (the January and March average) and report the gap monthly. July to September currently average 5.5.
3. **Pilot Spicy Taco House's offer and hours at Taco Time Express**, with a target of 12 orders a month against 7 today.
4. **Do not spend effort on menu rebalancing.** No taco type exceeds 25% and the spread is narrow.
5. **Label the filter on the page** so the 64 orders are not read as the whole business.

## Page 2: Revenue Analysis Report (filtered)
![Revenue Report](assets/Revenue_Report.PNG)
### Insights

- **Revenue by month:** January $166, March $166, July $30, September $56. February shows 1 order and $2.46 of tips but no revenue (see QA review).
- **Tips fell faster than revenue.** January to September: tips $51.03 to $8.13 (-84%), revenue $166 to $56 (-66%).
- **Tip rate:** 30% in January, 23% in March, 14.5% in September.
- **Revenue per order rose from $6.03 to $8.00** between January and September, but September is 7 orders, so one or two large orders move the figure. Against last year, revenue per order is lower ($6.52 vs $6.81).
- **Restaurant trajectories differ in kind, not just size.**
  - *Spicy Taco House:* -32.5%, -5.9%, -53.2%, +164.7%. Resilient, but the September gain is from a $12.75 base.
  - *El Taco Loco:* +59.2%, then -57.3%, -78.9%, -78.0%. A one-month spike followed by sustained decline.
  - *Grande Tacos:* -39.1%, -48.4%, then no revenue in July or September.
  - *Urban Tacos:* -15.7%, +25.2%, then no revenue in July or September.
  - *Taco Time Express:* -32.4%, -90.3%, no revenue in July, -44.1% in September.
- **July and September revenue came from two or three outlets**: July from Spicy Taco House ($20) and El Taco Loco ($9); September from Spicy Taco House ($34), Taco Time Express ($18) and El Taco Loco ($5).

### Recommendations

1. **Find out what drove El Taco Loco's January spike** (promotion, event, new listing) and why it did not hold. Replicating a spike only helps if the cause is understood.
2. **Open a reactivation review for Urban Tacos and Grande Tacos.** Their prior-year July and September revenue was $185 combined and is now zero. Confirm whether they were live.
3. **Hold a menu and pricing review with Grande Tacos**, the only restaurant down in every active month.
4. **Investigate the September tip rate (14.5%)** by restaurant and delivery time, then compare with Spicy Taco House, which kept volume.
5. **Replace "each order is worth more" in the narrative** with "within 2025, revenue per order rose from $6.03 to $8.00 on a very small sample; year on year it is 4% lower".
6. **Show zero rather than blank** for restaurants with no orders, and label the -100.0% cells "no orders" to avoid misreading.

### Insights

- **Average delivery is steady.** 50.28 min vs 50.05 (+0.5%), while distance grew 6.8% (13.23 vs 12.39 km). Minutes per km improved from 4.04 to 3.80 (-5.9%).
- **The average hides a wide spread.** City-restaurant values seen on the map run from 22.0 min (El Taco Loco, Houston) to 78.0 min (Grande Tacos, Chicago).

- **Delivery time follows the market, not the brand.** El Taco Loco ranges from 22.0 to 64.0 minutes, a 42-minute gap across six cities. 11 of these 16 combinations are above 45 minutes.
- **Size mix:** Regular 37 orders (58%), Large 27 (42%).
- **Large premium by taco type** (*derived from chart labels, 4 to 10 orders per cell*):

  | Taco | Large per order | Regular per order | Premium | Large share of orders |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Chicken | $8.25 | $5.11 | +61% | 31% |
  | Beef | $8.60 | $5.71 | +50% | 42% |
  | Fish | $7.60 | $5.25 | +45% | 56% |
  | Veggie | $7.17 | $5.50 | +30% | 38% |
  | Pork | $7.57 | $6.71 | +13% | 50% |

### Recommendations

1. **Set a 45-minute standard by city** and report the percentage of orders within it. Start with Chicago (Grande Tacos), Phoenix (El Taco Loco) and San Antonio (Urban Tacos).
2. **Use Houston (22.0 min) and San Diego (32.0 min) El Taco Loco as internal benchmarks.** Compare dispatch, kitchen prep and delivery radius against Phoenix (64.0 min).
3. **Review delivery radius where distance and duration are both high.** Distance grew 6.8% year on year, which can widen the slow tail.
4. **Upsell Large on Chicken first.** It has the biggest premium (+61%) and the lowest Large share (31%). Test a prompt at checkout for two weeks.
5. **Redesign the map visual.** Pie slices of averages are hard to read. A city by restaurant matrix with conditional formatting against 45 minutes would make the slow cells obvious.
6. **Retitle the narrative** from "delivery speed is stable" to "average delivery is stable; city-level times range from 22 to 78 minutes".
